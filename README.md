## AI & Medical Imaging Projects

### 1. SonaDx — Ultrasound Image Analysis

**Purpose:** Explore deep learning for classifying breast ultrasound images into normal, benign, and malignant categories.

**Work described:** Development of CNN models, including Xception, MobileNet, and ResNet, with Grad-CAM visualizations to help explain model predictions.

**Technologies:** Python, TensorFlow, convolutional neural networks, Grad-CAM.

**Skills demonstrated:** Medical image processing, model development, classification, and explainable AI.

### 2. BoneSight AI — Bone Image Classification

**Purpose:** Explore AI-assisted classification of bone images into normal and malignant categories.

**Project scope:** Image preprocessing, CNN model comparisons, and prediction results with confidence scores. Project documentation describes X-ray classification and active-learning experiments.

**Application features described:** Multiple-image upload, camera capture, authentication, guest access, prediction history, and API-based inference.

**Skills demonstrated:** Deep learning, image classification, active learning, and AI application integration.

### 3. ChestVision AI — Explainable Chest X-ray Analysis

**Purpose:** Develop an explainable web system for multi-class chest X-ray classification.

**Latest proposed scope:** Normal, bacterial pneumonia, viral pneumonia, COVID-19, tuberculosis, and emphysema.

**Planned features:** Image upload, preprocessing, model predictions, class probabilities, and Grad-CAM visual explanations.

**Proposed technologies:** Python, TensorFlow/Keras or PyTorch, CNN models, Flask or FastAPI.

**Skills demonstrated by the project plan:** AI system design, medical image classification, explainability, and web/API integration.

**Status:** Proposal and development planning; completed implementation and measured results are not established in the available history.

### 4. KidneySight AI — Kidney CT Image Analysis

**Purpose:** Develop an AI system to classify kidney CT images into normal, cyst, stone, and tumor categories.

**Dataset discussed:** CT Kidney Dataset containing 12,446 images.

**Planned features:** Image upload and preview, predicted class, confidence and class probabilities, Grad-CAM visualizations, and downloadable reports.

**Proposed technologies:** Python, EfficientNetB3, ResNet50, FastAPI, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.

**Skills demonstrated by the project plan:** Multi-class classification, model evaluation, explainable AI, and full-stack system design.

**Status:** Proposal and development planning; completed implementation and measured results are not established in the available history.

*Medical imaging projects are educational and research prototypes. They are not certified clinical diagnostic products.*
